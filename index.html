Five places to say it on the sand

16 guests in one rental 3 nights · Tue Mar 16 to Fri Mar 19, 2027 Flights from Salt Lake City Beachfront or a few minutes' walk

Ranked flight-first from a sweep of roughly 400 Airbnb and VRBO listings and 35 airports across Mexico, Central America, the Caribbean and Hawaii. With 16 tickets to buy, the fare is the biggest line, so the order follows the realistic mid-March fare from Salt Lake City, then the rental. Every option sleeps 16 or more, rates 4.8 or better, and is on or within a couple of minutes of a beach. Hawaii's cheap fares were tested and dropped: no legal 16-person beach rental there comes in under $7,400 before tax.

Lodging prices are the Airbnb totals shown for 16 adults on Sep 28, 2026, and usually exclude local lodging tax. Fares are per-person round-trip planning estimates for mid-March, not quotes, and exclude about $80 for a checked bag. The one-stop fare assumes a single connection with a layover under 3.5 hours.

How the numbers were built

  • Lodging is the total Airbnb displayed for 16 adults, Mar 16 to 19. Expect roughly 16 to 19 percent tax on top in Mexico and El Salvador, and 7 to 11 percent in Puerto Rico.
  • Fares come from trailing-year route averages nudged up for spring break. Treat them as plus or minus 20 percent until Delta's winter schedule loads in Oct to Nov 2026. Los Cabos and Puerto Vallarta are the only routes where March is one of the cheapest months.
  • Dates matter less than expected. Mar 17 to 20 priced flat to 10 percent higher across the board. Holy Week is Mar 21 to 28 and Mexican travel surges from Fri Mar 19, so return Thursday or lock Friday seats early.
  • Skipped on purpose: Kona, Costa Rica, Cozumel, Punta Cana, Belize and Nicaragua all show March as their most expensive month to fly. Volaris via Guadalajara is cheapest on paper but only flies SLC on Mon, Wed and Fri.

Before booking

  • Message the host about a ceremony and amplified music. Only options 2 and 5 say yes in writing.
  • Google Flights caps a search at 9 passengers and never shows Southwest. For 16 seats on one plane, get quotes from Delta's group desk (800-532-4777, deposit required) and Alaska's (800-445-4435, no deposit, free name changes to 3 days out).
  • Confirm where each guest flies from. The nonstop advantage for Puerto Vallarta and Cancún only exists from SLC.
